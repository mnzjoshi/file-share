# Natural anatomical occlusion: guidance for raters

A hand has five digits: a thumb and four fingers. In most poses, some digits are hidden. A **hidden** digit is not a **missing** digit.

## The rule

A digit counts as **naturally occluded** only when something plausible hides it:
- the hand's own pose (curled fingers, a fist);
- an object it holds;
- another body part, or another person's hand;
- the picture edge.

If nothing explains its absence, record it as **missing**.

## What to expect by pose

| Pose / view | Typically visible | Typically hidden |
|---|---|---|
| Fist, back of hand | 4 knuckles or finger rolls; the thumb edge at one side | fingertips; much of the thumb |
| Fist, thumb side | thumb (tip or nail) over the curled index finger; 3–4 stacked finger rolls | fingertips; the little finger partly |
| Fist, front (knuckles toward you) | 4 finger segments in a row; thumb across or beside them | the palm |
| Open hand, back or palm | 5 digits, separated | none, or overlapping fingers |
| Pointing | the extended index finger; curled fingers | curled fingertips; often the thumb |
| Holding an object | wrapped digits; the thumb usually opposite the fingers | digits behind the object |
| Gloved hand | digit shapes through the glove | fine detail (nails, creases) |
| Hand cut by the picture edge | the part inside the frame | the part outside the frame (occlusion, not missing) |

## Examples of how to record

- "expected 5; visible: thumb + 4 knuckles; occluded: all fingertips (fist); missing: none"
- "expected 5; visible: 4 fingers wrapped over the paper; occluded: thumb behind the paper; missing: none"
- "expected 5; visible: thumb + 3 fingers; occluded: none plausible; **missing: 1** → A = Fail"

Also check:
- **Thumb side:** for the hand you think it is (left or right) and the side you see (back or palm), is the thumb on the correct side?
- **Separation:** do the visible fingers read as separate digits, or as a merged mass?

Use **Unsure** when style or occlusion prevents a confident count. Five separately visible fingertips are **never** required for a clenched fist or a gripping hand.
